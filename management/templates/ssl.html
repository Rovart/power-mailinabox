TLS (SSL) Certificates

A TLS (formerly called SSL) certificate is a cryptographic file that proves to anyone connecting to a web address that the connection is secure between you and the owner of that address. You need a TLS certificate for this box’s hostname ({{hostname}}) and every other domain name and subdomain that this box is hosting a website for (see the list below).
