Services

Mail, DNS and this control panel are always on. The optional services below can be turned off if you don’t use them: they are then no longer served and their background jobs stop. Their data is kept (and still backed up) and they keep being updated, so you can turn them back on at any time.

You can also turn services on and off on the box itself with sudo management/cli.py system services enable|disable nextcloud|zpush|webmail|munin.
