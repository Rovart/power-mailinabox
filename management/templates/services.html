Services

Mail, DNS and this control panel are always on. The optional services below can be turned off if you don’t use them: they are then no longer served and their background jobs stop. Their data is kept (and still backed up) and they keep being updated, so you can turn them back on at any time.

You can also turn services on and off on the box itself with sudo management/cli.py system services enable|disable nextcloud|zpush|webmail|munin. If you’d rather not install and update the web applications you turned off, run sudo management/services.py update-turned-off no: the setup then skips them, and one turned back on is served after the next sudo mailinabox.

Access from the internet

On a box exposed to the internet, you can make web applications you only use from the box itself (through an SSH tunnel) or from trusted networks (e.g. your VPN or office) unavailable to everybody else: their requests are refused with 403 Forbidden. Mail apps (IMAP, POP, SMTP), DNS, mail autoconfiguration and your websites are not affected.
